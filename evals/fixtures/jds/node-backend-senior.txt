Senior Backend Engineer
Kaya Financial — Bonifacio Global City, Taguig (Hybrid, 3 days onsite)

About the role
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join the payments platform team.
You will own services that move money, which means correctness, observability
and careful failure handling matter more here than raw feature velocity.

What you will do
- Design, build and operate Node.js services in TypeScript.
- Model data in PostgreSQL and keep queries fast as tables grow past 100M rows.
- Work with Kubernetes and Terraform to ship and scale those services.
- Instrument services so failures are visible before customers report them.
- Participate in a shared on-call rotation.

What we are looking for
- 5+ years of backend engineering experience.
- Strong Node.js and TypeScript.
- Deep PostgreSQL knowledge, including indexing and query tuning.
- Experience with Kubernetes in production.
- Experience with Terraform or comparable infrastructure-as-code.
- Familiarity with message queues such as Kafka or RabbitMQ.
- Comfortable with Docker and CI/CD pipelines.

Nice to have
- Payments or fintech background.
- Experience with gRPC.
